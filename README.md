# Data Science / Consumer Insights Extern — Beats by Dre via Extern

Analyzed ~1,300 Amazon over-ear headphone reviews to identify product strengths, customer pain points, and competitive positioning opportunities for Beats.

* Compared Beats Studio Pro and Solo 4 with Sony, Bose, Sennheiser, and Soundcore using quantitative and qualitative review analysis.



* Identified Beats’ strongest category advantages in Bluetooth (+0.324), ANC (+0.132), battery (+0.081), and noise performance (+0.073), while uncovering weaker customer sentiment around the app (-0.335), bass (-0.257), fit (-0.095), design (-0.087), and comfort (-0.059).



* Found Studio Pro to be the stronger premium product anchor, while Solo 4 showed a greater concentration of customer concerns.



* Identified recurring customer friction around clamp force, ear-cup size, heat/sweat, limited app/EQ controls, and perceived value at full retail pricing.



* Translated findings into a product and positioning recommendation: strengthen trust, comfort, and perceived value before expanding the headphone strategy, while positioning Studio Pro around “everyday premium audio.”



* Presented findings through data visualizations and an executive-style strategic presentation.
